🕰️ WHEN INVESTING USUALLY FITS BETTER

🧯 IF THE MARKET FALLS

Pause before reacting. Do not borrow more just to chase the loss. Check whether your time horizon and reason for investing have changed, and protect essential cash first.

📉
Normal market loss?

Markets can fall. Avoid panic decisions, review what you own and why, and remember that recovery can take time. A diversified long-term approach is different from trying to win back money quickly.

🚨
Think it was a scam?

Contact your bank or payment provider quickly, keep screenshots and payment details, and report it through official channels.
