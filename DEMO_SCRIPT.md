# FinPulse 90-second live demo

1. **Scam Check** — paste a short public money video link. Let FinPulse fill the transcript; if the platform blocks it, use the saved transcript fallback.
2. Click **Check for warning signs**. Show the exact words that triggered the warning, what seems to be sold, who may benefit, and the plain-English next step.
3. Click **See what this could mean for my money**.
4. In **Money Lab**, use £500, £100/month, 5 years, long-term/spare savings, emergency savings = yes, high-interest debt = no. Show the market paths and the 30% fall illustration.
5. Switch the purpose to **rent or bills** and rerun to show how the readiness message changes because the money is needed soon.
6. Open **Money Battles** for the return-use case: users come back to compete on judgement and scam spotting, not fake profit.

Close: **“FinPulse helps young people check the content they see, make risk visible before money is real, and learn the pattern so they are harder to scam next time.”**
